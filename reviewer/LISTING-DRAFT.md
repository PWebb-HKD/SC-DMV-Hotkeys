**Name:** SC DMV Hotkeys

**Short description:** Keyboard-driven SCDMV record workflows, PDF tools and status alerts, with a built-in synthetic practice mode.

**Single purpose:** Assist authorized users with SCDMV member record searches and related report review/data entry through user-invoked shortcuts, status summaries and PDF tools.

**Description:** SC DMV Hotkeys automates user-requested plate, driver/customer and same-residence searches for authorized SCDMV accounts. Review records in a continuous PDF viewer with selectable text and original-PDF printing, inspect license/tag alerts and suspension details, follow registered-owner customer numbers, manage a session queue and generate editable PDF417 data-entry barcodes. Optional voice alerts, background query windows, website/PDF colors and a shortcut menu support the same workflow. A disclosed, self-contained Demo Mode provides fictional records and SCUTTIES examples without a live account or server. The extension does not grant SCDMV access, supply government records independently, or replace review of the original report.

**Distribution requested in the project:** Unlisted / anyone with the installation link. Confirm the dashboard currently offers the desired visibility and that its review requirements are met.

## Privacy form preparation

The extension processes credentials, identifiers, names, addresses, report/photo/violation information and report-derived data locally to perform the requested workflows. Live searches necessarily transmit the requested query/login to SCDMV. Optional saved login/blacklist uses a locally encrypted vault; unlocked information, history, report snapshots and queues use browser-session storage. Preferences are stored locally, not through Chrome sync. No developer-operated analytics, advertising, sale/sharing endpoint or remote extension-code loader is implemented. Local voices/packaged audio are user-enabled.
